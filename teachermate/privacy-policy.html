Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 1, 2025

Teacher Mate's Use and Transfer of Information: Teacher Mate's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs (including Google Calendar API and Google Tasks API) adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Teacher Mate ("we", "our", or "the Application") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Windows desktop application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information from Google

When you sign in with your Google account, the Application accesses:

  • Your Google account email address (for account identification)
  • Your basic profile information (name and profile picture, displayed within the app)

2.2 Calendar and Tasks Data from Google

When you connect Google Calendar and Google Tasks, the Application accesses:

  • Calendar event titles, descriptions, and locations
  • Event start and end times
  • Calendar names and settings
  • Event reminders and notifications
  • Task lists and task items (titles, descriptions, due dates, completion status)

2.3 Classroom Data (Stored Locally)

Teacher Mate stores the following data locally on your device for classroom management purposes:

  • Student names, photos, and group assignments
  • Weekly timetable and lesson progress records
  • Seating chart arrangements
  • Counseling records and personal teaching notes
  • Class photo album layouts
  • Attendance and cleaning duty records

2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics
  • We do not collect device information or system data
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies
  • We do not collect information about other applications on your device

3. How We Use Your Information

Your Google account and calendar data is used exclusively for:

  • Authentication: Verifying your identity via Google Sign-In
  • Display: Showing your calendar events and tasks in the application interface
  • Synchronization: Keeping your local calendar and tasks in sync with Google Calendar and Google Tasks
  • Management: Allowing you to create, edit, complete, and delete events and tasks
  • Offline Access: Storing data locally so you can view events and tasks without internet
We do NOT:
  • Share your data with any third parties
  • Use your data for advertising or marketing
  • Analyze your calendar or classroom data for any purpose
  • Train AI models or machine learning algorithms with your data
  • Sell or monetize your information in any way

4. Data Storage

4.1 Local Storage

All classroom data and Google Calendar data is stored locally on your Windows device. The data is accessible only by your Windows user account. No external cloud storage is used by Teacher Mate itself.

  • Classroom data is stored under Documents\TeacherMate\
  • Data is accessible only by your Windows user account

4.2 OneDrive Backup (Optional)

Teacher Mate offers an optional backup feature using Microsoft OneDrive. This backup is performed entirely through your own Microsoft account and is fully under your control. Teacher Mate does not operate any backup servers.

4.3 No Remote Storage

We do not operate any servers or cloud storage. Your data never leaves your device except when:

  • Synchronizing directly with the Google Calendar API or Google Tasks API
  • You explicitly choose to back up to your own OneDrive

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

The Application communicates only with Google APIs for authentication, calendar, and tasks synchronization. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

5.1 Google Calendar and Google Tasks Integration

When you authorize Google Calendar and Google Tasks access:

  • Data is transmitted directly between your device and Google servers
  • We do not act as an intermediary or store your credentials
  • Your Google authentication tokens are stored locally on your device
  • You can revoke access anytime through Google Account settings

6. Data Security

We implement the following measures to protect your data:

  • Authentication: OAuth 2.0 for secure Google account access
  • No Password Storage: We never see or store your Google password
  • Local Isolation: Classroom data is stored locally and not transmitted to any external server by Teacher Mate
  • Minimal Scope: Only the Google API scopes necessary for the described features are requested

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Access and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • View: All your data is visible within the application
  • Delete: Uninstalling the application removes all local data
  • Disconnect: You can disconnect your Google account at any time from within the app

7.2 Revoking Google Access

To revoke Teacher Mate's access to your Google account:

  1. Visit Google Account Permissions
  2. Find "Teacher Mate" in the list
  3. Click "Remove Access"

8. Data Retention

  • Classroom data is retained locally until you delete it or uninstall the application
  • Uninstalling the application permanently deletes all locally stored data
  • Disconnecting your Google account does not delete locally cached calendar data (manual deletion required)

9. Children's Privacy

Teacher Mate is intended for use by teachers and school staff. It is not directed at users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International Data Transfers

Classroom data is stored locally on your device. When synchronizing with Google Calendar or Google Tasks, data transfer is handled by Google in accordance with their privacy policy.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the application after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Compliance

This application complies with:

  • Google API Services User Data Policy
  • Google OAuth 2.0 Policies
  • Limited Use requirements for Google user data
  • Google Calendar API Terms of Service
  • Google Tasks API Terms of Service
